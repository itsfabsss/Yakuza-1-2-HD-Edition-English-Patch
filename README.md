<h1 align="center">Yakuza 1 & 2 HD Edition — English Patch</h1>

<p align="center">
  An English patch for the PS3 and Wii U versions of <i>Ryu ga Gotoku 1&2 HD Edition</i>,<br>
  based on Sylwahan's <i>Yakuza Restored</i> and <i>Yakuza 2 Restored</i> PlayStation 2 patches.
</p>

## Features

- Full English script for both games, ported from the *Restored* patches (story cutscenes, street dialogue, substories and menus)
- The original PS2 English font, redrawn for HD
- Battle Cards and other textures recreated in higher resolution
